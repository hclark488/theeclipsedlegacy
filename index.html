ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Heather J. Clark

Heather J. Clark is an author with a love for stories filled with magic, mystery, complicated characters, and worlds where nothing is quite what it seems.

She is the author of The Eclipsed Legacy, a fantasy series born from her love of immersive worlds, hidden histories, dangerous magic, and characters who have a habit of asking questions they probably shouldn’t.
