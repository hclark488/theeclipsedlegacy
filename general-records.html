General Records

Official public cartographic records of the Kingdom of Veyr.

CROWN RECORD // BF-01

Bellfall Academy

CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC

An ancient academic institution in northeastern Eastreach, where students bearing recognized Marks are trained within Veyr's Five Orders. Bellfall stands beyond the highlands near the eastern sea, serving generations of Marked students under Crown-recognized tradition.

REGION: EASTREACH STATUS: ACTIVE RECOGNIZED ORDERS: FIVE

CROWN RECORD // OR-01

Orinth

CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC

The royal capital of Veyr and seat of the Crown. Orinth is the kingdom's center of government, commerce, and transportation, connected to the wider kingdom through major Crownways and Wayline routes.

REGION: HEARTLANDS STATUS: CROWN SEAT ACCESS: PUBLIC

CROWN RECORD // EW-01

The Elderwood

CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC

A vast old-growth forest region of north-central Veyr, crossed by rivers, old roads, timber routes, and long-established settlements. Portions of the deeper forest remain difficult to survey and maintain.

REGION: ELDERWOOD STATUS: MAPPED PRIMARY ROUTES: ROAD / RIVER

CROWN RECORD // GW-01

Greyward

CLASSIFICATION: RESTRICTED

A Crown recovery hospital located deep within the Crownspine Mountains of Northreach. Greyward provides specialized long-term care and treatment in a secure and remote setting.

REGION: NORTHREACH STATUS: ACTIVE CROWN ACCESS: RESTRICTED

CROWN RECORD // WL-01

The Wetlands

CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC

A vast western region of marshes, flooded forests, deltas, reeds, and waterways. Boats, ferries, and barges form essential transportation networks where conventional roads become impractical.

REGION: WETLANDS STATUS: MAPPED PRIMARY ROUTES: WATERWAY / FERRY

CROWN RECORD // WR-01

The Westerlands

CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC

Veyr's western coastal territory, marked by rocky shores, fishing communities, maritime trade, and routes leading toward Vesperport and the offshore Veiled Isles.

REGION: WESTERLANDS STATUS: MAPPED PRIMARY ROUTES: COASTAL / MARITIME

CROWN RECORD // SV-01

Sunvale

CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC

The warm and fertile southern region of Veyr, known for grain, orchards, vineyards, livestock, river trade, and extensive agricultural production.

REGION: SUNVALE STATUS: MAPPED PRIMARY INDUSTRY: AGRICULTURE

CROWN RECORD // ER-01

Eastreach

CLASSIFICATION: PUBLIC

A broad eastern region stretching from inland crossroads and foothills to rugged northeastern highlands and the Eastern Sea. Eastreach contains major trade routes, coastal settlements, and some of Veyr's most difficult overland terrain.

REGION: EASTREACH STATUS: MAPPED PRIMARY ROUTES: ROAD / COASTAL
